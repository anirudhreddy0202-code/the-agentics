# Holiday Shopping Trends – 2023 Snapshot

- **E-commerce surge:** Online holiday sales rose 12% year over year to $210 billion in 2023, driven by faster shipping and smoother mobile checkout. [source needed]

- **Earlier buying:** 68% of shoppers started looking for gifts before Black Friday, and 42% made at least one major purchase in November, extending the season beyond a single weekend. [source needed]

- **Experience-focused gifting:** 54% of consumers would spend more on travel, concerts, or subscriptions than on physical products—a rise of eight points since 2021. [source needed]

- **Sustainability influence:** 39% say a brand's environmental record affects their holiday decisions, and 22% actively seek recycled or upcycled items. [source needed]

- **Omni-channel advantage:** Retailers offering both in-store pickup and same-day delivery see a 15% larger average basket than those with a single fulfillment option. [source needed]

These trends suggest that convenience, early planning, experiential gifts, and sustainability will shape holiday buying in 2024, while supply-chain volatility and the durability of experience-based gifting remain open questions. [source needed]
