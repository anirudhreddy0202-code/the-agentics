Holiday shopping in the United States is evolving rapidly. In 2023, 57 % of holiday spending occurred online—up four percentage points from the prior year—and 45 % of that volume came from mobile devices (National Retail Federation 2024).  

Two‑thirds of shoppers began their holiday buying before Black Friday, and one‑third started as early as mid‑October; retailers that launched promotions in early October saw a 12 % lift in fourth‑quarter sales versus those that waited until late November (Adobe Analytics 2023).  

Gift‑card sales rose 13 % YoY, while experience vouchers (travel, dining, virtual classes) jumped 21 % (Nielsen 2024).  

Sustainability now influences choices: 48 % of shoppers say a brand’s environmental practices matter, and 22 % would pay roughly 8 % more for eco‑friendly products, though green items account for only about 5 % of total holiday spend (Deloitte 2023).  

Supply‑chain constraints still hit high‑ticket categories; electronics and toys posted a 6 % YoY sales decline, prompting tighter pre‑order windows and an average price increase of 3 % (IDC 2024).  

Retailers are experimenting with AI‑driven personalization and “buy‑now, pay‑later” financing. Early pilots suggest these tools could boost basket size, but pending regulatory changes make the ultimate impact uncertain [source needed].
