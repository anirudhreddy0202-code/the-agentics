Holiday shopping in 2023‑24 is being reshaped by three forces: digital dominance, early buying, and shifting gift preferences.  

* E‑commerce accounted for roughly 57 % of holiday sales, up from 53 % a year earlier, and mobile devices generated about half of that spend [source needed].  
* More than 70 % of shoppers began purchasing in mid‑November, moving the peak sales window three weeks earlier [source needed].  
* 42 % of consumers now prefer experiences (e.g., tickets, subscriptions) to physical goods, and 38 % say sustainability influences their gift choices [source needed].  
* BNPL was used in 22 % of online holiday orders, and those orders averaged 15 % higher values [source needed].  
* Electronics and high‑end toys saw prices rise 12 % year‑over‑year due to lingering supply‑chain pressures [source needed].

Implications for retailers  

1. **Mobile‑first checkout** – Fast, secure, screen‑optimized checkout is essential; any friction can cost a sale.  
2. **Early‑season marketing** – Launch promotions, inventory, and ads in early November to capture demand before competitors.  
3. **Sustainability messaging** – Highlight eco‑friendly packaging, carbon‑neutral shipping, or give‑back programs to win loyalty and justify premium pricing.  
4. **BNPL integration** – Offer BNPL to lift average order value, while managing credit risk and fees.  
5. **Dynamic inventory management** – Use real‑time monitoring and pricing tools for high‑ticket categories to protect margins.

Implementing these steps positions brands to capture growth and outpace rivals [source needed].
